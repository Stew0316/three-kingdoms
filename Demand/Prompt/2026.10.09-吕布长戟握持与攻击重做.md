## 已落地动作

| 状态 | 当前实现 |
| --- | --- |
| 站立 | 双手在胸腰之间分开握持，戟头朝前上方，手不举在肩后等待投掷 |
| 移动 | 主手在腰侧近水平后拖长戟，副手自然摆动，起停平滑切换握柄权重 |
| 蓄势 | 沉胯、腰肩向后带动，双手把戟收到身后 |
| 挥击 | 背后→上方→前方切入→压低随势；明确分段弧线，避免最短角插值绕错方向 |
| 收势 | 刃端先完成随势，身体和双手再回低位警戒，武器始终留在手里 |
| 普攻/突进 | 缩短收戟幅度，仍沿劈扫弧线，不再以线性前送表现所有攻击 |
| 受击 | 按实际击退方向在当前朝向内收身，保留轻微挤压与偏移 |

`CharacterPoseConfig` 和六份 `lubu-*-pose.tres` 保存实际关键姿态。只使用一套骨架；参考分支将武器从手链中解放，再由双臂追踪两个握点。魏延不使用该分支。原来的战斗数值与动作窗口不变。

## 生图方式与成功配件的最终提示词

方式：内置 imagegen；输入参考 `Source/People/LvBu/lubu-pose-study-v3.png`。输出复制到项目目录，保留原始 alpha；未切换 CLI/API。

```text
Create a genuinely transparent-background two-column modular accessory sprite sheet faithfully extracted/reconstructed from the attached Lu Bu three-pose reference. Match the reference's realistic painted style, exact equipment design and muted colors; no redesign, no person, no text or grid. LEFT COLUMN: the complete full-length Fangtian halberd upright, silver spear and crescent blades at top, long straight thin black shaft occupying 75% of the whole weapon length, antique gold details, small red tassel, butt spike at bottom. All tips fully inside frame. RIGHT COLUMN: the two very long red crown pheasant plumes as a single isolated piece, matching the reference arches: both roots together at bottom-right, shafts rise upward and arch toward left, tapering feather tips trail down-left. No crown or head. Keep both accessories fully contained in their separate halves, with clear transparent gutters and alpha outside artwork. Do not shorten the pole or flip the feather roots.
```

实际结果：透明背景有效；双翎带有小金色根座，绑定时与原冠叠合；杆身比例未严格达到提示的75%，保留完整图片并以实际外观验收，不将提示词当作已实现的事实。
