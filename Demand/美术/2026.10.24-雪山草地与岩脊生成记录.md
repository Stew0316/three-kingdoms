## 雪山最终提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production environment sprite for a 2D top-down Three Kingdoms China adventure game. Generate one imposing, broad snow mountain massif, true transparent alpha background, landscape 1536x1024. Orthographic elevated three-quarter overhead game view, NO horizon and NO sky. Ancient, harsh, cold, restrained painterly realism, very detailed heavy charcoal slate rock, believable jagged layered ridges, weathered fractures, thick cold white snow collecting on ledges, subtle blue-grey shadow, realistic natural geology, NOT low-poly, NOT simple vector or cartoon. Mountain occupies most frame with safe transparent margins. Highest snow peaks in upper third. Wide connected steep impassable foothill cliffs across lower third. Exactly ONE small dark natural cave entrance at horizontal center of mountain's front base (near 50% x, 84% y), with a broad readable snowy ground apron leading straight down toward bottom. Cave should be a small grounded opening not a gigantic fantasy portal. The whole mountain silhouette isolated, including irregular rocky foot and scattered scree, edges smoothly transparent, no rectangular ground panel, no checkerboard, no text, no symbols, no buildings, no people. Soft cold daylight from upper left. High material detail retained at game zoom. This is scenery with only its foothill entrance approachable, not a climbable mountain.
