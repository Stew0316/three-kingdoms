## 无图片冠翎

`ProceduralPlume2D.cs` 从每条十个控制点生成三段连续贝塞尔曲线，描述拱起、下垂和尾端回卷。每条 144 个纵向分段、6 个横向分段，共 1015 顶点、1728 三角形。轮廓有收尖与羽枝边缘变化，`ProceduralPlume.gdshader` 用数学纹理绘制暗红羽枝和明暗；代码没有纹理采样，网格的 Texture 为 null。

两条网格分别有自己的形状、宽度、材质、频率、幅度和相位。当前前后翎宽度为 14.7／11.2，运动速率 0.75／1.13，摆幅 5.0／6.3，初相 0.4／3.1。共享时间源仅用于跟随游戏暂停，各根并不同时同幅运动。

根部位移严格为零；上段只分配小部分柔性，主要形变在后段逐渐展开，下垂和回卷尾梢摆幅更大。外轮廓在静止时也保持卷曲，避免只在运动时才像参考。两根固定在冠骨，冠座与头部在其前方遮挡连接处。人物和盔甲不接入羽带变形。

起停读取碰撞后的实际速度：移动时逐渐加风，停步收稳后权重归零、波形时钟停止；顶墙不持续假飘，暂停冻结。当前是可控的程序变形，不是带碰撞的真实羽毛动力学。也没有加入未被要求的常驻环境风。

## 动作衔接

- 站立采用主手斜持戟、副手垂在身侧；移动仍有拖戟姿态，攻击生效时双手接柄。
- 接柄由肩前方弧线连接自由手与握点，避免直线穿肩造成手肘折叠；原握点与骨长约束仍保留。
- 反击从实际姿态按反击时钟采样，不再叠加会改变握持路径的低通滞后；零增量帧也保存首次反击起势，避免默认零姿态导致武器跳位。
- 调整前送斩入握点，避开副手挤肩。动作关键点与姿态可以改变，技能伤害、冷却和命中窗口未改变。

## V4 身体完整生成提示词

输入：`LvBuReferenceV3.png`，作为身份与造型参考。

```text
Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset type: production 2D skeletal character sprite atlas, transparent RGBA.
Input image 1 is the EXACT approved full-body Lu Bu character design to preserve. Create detached animation parts of this SAME character, not a new design. Match the painted dark iron and restrained gold armor shapes, stern square face, thick limbs and broad boots precisely. Non-pixel painted game art, visible subtle painterly grain, NO shiny toy plastic.
Output a 1536x1536 square with exactly 4 columns x 4 rows of well-separated objects, each object completely inside its cell with transparent padding. No labels or grid.
Row 1: head including hair and black/gold/red-jewel crown BUT NO RED PLUMES; chest cuirass with red neck scarf and knot, stop before belt, NO arms/shoulder sockets; waist with round gold buckle/red sash and side skirt plates; single dark crimson cape.
Row 2: screen-left shoulder pauldron plus thick upper arm; screen-left armored forearm ONLY; screen-right shoulder pauldron plus thick upper arm; screen-right armored forearm ONLY.
Row 3: screen-left upper leg with overlapping thigh plate and dark trousers; screen-left shin and broad armored boot; screen-right upper leg with thigh plate and trousers; screen-right shin and broad armored boot.
Row 4: screen-left gripping gauntlet hand; screen-right relaxed closed gauntlet hand; complete full-length black-shaft halberd oriented blade UP, includes silver blade/gold fittings and red tassel; EMPTY transparent cell.
All limbs should be nearly vertical in the atlas, connection direction from TOP to BOTTOM, SAME three-quarter facing-right view as reference. Broad muscular upper arms and heavy forearms, NOT skinny rods. Elbows, wrists, hips and knees should have solid dark flexible under-armor extensions for overlap: absolutely NO circular hollow sockets, NO exposed tube holes, NO detachable toy ball-joints. Boots large and proportional, both toe tips toward screen right. Preserve the exact shoulder crest shapes from reference and long angular skirt plates. Head expression, eye size and hair silhouette should match the input, no face simplification. Individual parts are disconnected, not a posed paper doll. Every part fully visible and separate, with generous margins, do not add plume graphics anywhere. Truly transparent background with actual alpha, no black background, no ground shadows, no lettering.
```
